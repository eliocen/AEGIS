## Initial scientific scope

The first AEGIS-Vision problem is deliberately narrow: **image authenticity and manipulation detection research**.

The lineage may investigate authentic-versus-manipulated image evidence, manipulation/deepfake detection where the subsequently frozen dataset taxonomy supports it, and visual evidence quality/provenance signals.

Step1 does not claim or authorize contextual-misuse detection, cross-modal text-image consistency, video or audio authenticity, full multimodal integration, production readiness, autonomous attribution, or operational/national-security effectiveness.

## Prospective questions

**H1 鈥?performance hypothesis:** a frozen AEGIS-Vision candidate can exceed a prospectively defined image-only baseline on prospectively frozen image-authenticity/manipulation metrics.

**H2 鈥?observability hypothesis:** the candidate can provide non-degenerate visual evidence outputs under prospectively frozen observability criteria.

Both hypotheses are `PROSPECTIVE_UNDECIDED`. No result is claimed.

Numerical metrics, thresholds, baselines, datasets, splits, populations, and decision rules are intentionally not invented in Step1. They must be frozen before implementation or formal evaluation.

## Mandatory governance sequence

1. Freeze dataset, task, population, provenance, taxonomy, and split rules.
2. Freeze image-only baselines and architecture/evidence-interface design.
3. Freeze metrics, numerical thresholds, observability criteria, and hypothesis decision rules.
4. Authorize an exact implementation allowlist.
5. Implement only the authorized boundary.
6. Verify implementation and invariants before training.
7. Freeze training configuration and evidence-output contract.
8. Conduct training only after explicit authorization.
9. Freeze formal evaluation protocol before evaluating.
10. Interpret H1/H2 only from frozen evidence and close the lineage or authorize a separately governed successor.

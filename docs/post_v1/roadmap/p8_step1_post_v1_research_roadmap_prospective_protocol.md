## Purpose

P8 documents future AEGIS research without retrospectively expanding the frozen AEGIS Research Core v1.0. The immutable baseline remains `v1.0.0-research-core` at commit `39492cf6d93987fdf9ebee29b95fdb826b8391e4`.

A roadmap item is a research direction, not evidence that the capability already exists.

## Mandatory scientific inheritance

The v1.0 history remains evidence. In particular, M3 and M4 remain `NOT_SUPPORTED`, the v0.35 posterior-context correction remains `CORRECTION_NOT_SUPPORTED`, and v0.36 active-intervention robustness remains `NOT_COMPUTED`.

Future work must not infer classifier non-robustness from selector failure. It must not relabel `NOT_COMPUTED` as a numerical result.

Production readiness and autonomous attribution remain not established. Official test access remains separately governed.

## Prospective governance rule

Before a future track changes implementation or performs scientific evaluation, that track requires its own prospective protocol. The protocol must freeze the relevant research question, hypothesis, dataset/task/population, modality, metrics, thresholds, evidence provenance, execution authorization, and stopping/decision rules as applicable.

New results belong to a separately governed post-v1 experimental lineage. They do not mutate the v1.0 release.

## Evidence states

Roadmap items may be `PROPOSED`, `PROTOCOL_REQUIRED`, `IMPLEMENTATION_AUTHORIZED`, `EVALUATION_AUTHORIZED`, `SUPPORTED`, `NOT_SUPPORTED`, `NOT_COMPUTED`, or `DEFERRED`. These states must not be conflated.

## P8 sequencing principle

The roadmap should favor dependency-aware progression rather than claiming all modalities at once. Constituent modality research and evidence contracts should precede full multimodal integration.

## Step1 execution boundary

No future track is implemented in Step1. No model/source changes, model execution, training, new evaluation, or official-test access occur.

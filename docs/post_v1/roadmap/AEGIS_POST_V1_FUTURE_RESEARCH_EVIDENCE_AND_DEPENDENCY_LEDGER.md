## Dependency interpretation

A dependency is not an authorization. It identifies evidence or contracts that must exist before a later research claim can be made.

T6 is intentionally downstream. Full multimodal integration should not be treated as a single implementation milestone before its constituent modality and cross-modal evidence contracts exist.

T7 may proceed as a separate mechanistic research line, but it cannot erase or reinterpret the frozen selector-discrimination failure. Any new selector/control mechanism needs a new prospective protocol.

T8 remains human-centered analyst decision support unless a later, separately governed body of evidence supports a broader claim.

## Evidence discipline

Future tracks must preserve prospective hypotheses, dataset/task/population boundaries, metrics and thresholds, execution authorization, negative results, source/evidence hashes, and immutable release identities. Official-test access remains separately governed.
